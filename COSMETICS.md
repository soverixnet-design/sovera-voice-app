# SOVERA VIP collection

## Castle Flag arrival

Open **Me → Frames & effects → Castle Flag → Entry**. Preview the seven-second castle arrival, then tap **Use entry in preview** to play it on subsequent room visits in this session. The flag is drawn and animated natively over the bundled castle artwork; no HTML, VAP player, or network download is required. Reduced motion holds the flag still and uses the existing short automatic dismissal. Existing local-selection and account-inventory limitations below also apply to Castle Flag.

Open **Me → Frames & effects → Sovera VIP7** (the fourth theme).

- **Frame**: play a light sweep, then use it around your profile and occupied preview-room avatar. The applied frame loops while visible.
- **Entry**: preview the phoenix arrival or select it for the next room visit. It fades in, rises with gold particles and closes automatically after 3.2 seconds. The close control remains available.
- **Speaking glow**: preview the gold waveform ring, expanding pulses and orbiting particles. No microphone permission or audio processing is used.
- **Medal / Name tag**: animate and apply the Top Host artwork and Sovera Legend nameplate to the profile's Style preview. Verified rank and VIP values are unchanged.

Selections are local to the running app and reset when it restarts. Reset clears all four applied choices. These are cosmetic demonstrations, not purchases or granted account entitlements. Inventory storage, RTC voice detection and remote member arrival events are outside this change.

Flutter animates light, particles, opacity and position over five compressed WebP artworks; the phoenix wings do not deform. Native rendering avoids bundling a video or loading large animated files for every seat. System reduced-motion settings show still artwork and disable repeating animation; the arrival still closes after a short readable interval. Hidden routes pause their loops.

Source images were generated for this SOVERA collection. Files live in `assets/effects/`; the existing pubspec asset-directory registration includes them.
