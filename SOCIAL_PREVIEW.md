# Friendship, CP and Love design preview

Friendship uses a holding-hands icon and friends/requests tabs. CP shows mutual-consent guidance, two avatar positions and selectable frame colors. Love belongs to the CP concept and offers Heart, Rose and Starlight visual previews.

This release adds profile navigation and interactive design exploration only. Friend lookup, invitations, acceptance, relationship state, points, gifts, anniversaries and rankings are not connected. Invitation actions explicitly report that no request was sent. No social data, coin balance or relationship is changed. Authentication, participant-only access rules and mutual approval must be implemented and verified before activating these operations.
